Summoning sickness is a term for the rule that a creature cannot attack or use activated abilities either with the tap or untap smybol if it has not been continuously controlled by a player since the beginning of that player?s mot recent turn.
The stack is the game zone where spells and abilities are put when they are played and where they wait to resolve. The stack system allows players to "respond" to the actions of other players before those actions take full effect, enabling interactive gameplay even with "instantaneous" effects.
Technically, a spell is an object on the stack that exists before the spell takes effect. Players turn cards into spells by casting them, usually from non-land cards in their hand.
